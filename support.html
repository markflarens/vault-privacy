Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Vault Pro is billed through your Apple ID. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Vault to manage or cancel. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
How do I restore a previous purchase? Open Vault, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.
How do I delete my account and data? Open Vault, go to Settings and tap Delete Account, or email us at markoberladin@gmail.com and we'll remove your data.
Why does Vault ask for microphone access? Only to let you add income and expenses by voice. Your speech is converted to text to log the transaction — nothing else.
Is my financial data secure? Yes. Your data is encrypted and never sold. See our Privacy Policy for details.
